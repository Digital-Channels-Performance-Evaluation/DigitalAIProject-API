Tier: Poor + Trend: Declining    →  🔴 CRITICAL (Red email)
Tier: Poor + Trend: Stable       →  🟠 HIGH (Orange email)
Tier: Average + Trend: Declining →  🟡 MEDIUM (Yellow email)
Tier: Good + Trend: Stable       →  🟢 LOW (Green email)
Tier: Excellent + Trend: Improving → 🟢 LOW (Green email)
